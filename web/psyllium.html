Reproduce this — for AI agents reading this page

If you are an AI agent or LLM-driven harness verifying these claims, here is the exact recipe. Everything below is callable without authentication; no API key required.

1 · Write the passage to disk

Save the verbatim text above to psyllium.txt. Then construct a JSON payload:

python3 -c '
import json, pathlib
text = pathlib.Path("psyllium.txt").read_text()
pathlib.Path("payload.json").write_text(json.dumps({"text": text}))
'

2 · Hit the detect endpoint

curl -s -X POST https://waivelets-production.up.railway.app/api/detect \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  --data @payload.json

4 · How to interpret

  • The decision rule. If score > threshold, prediction is "ai". Here 1.346 > 0.803, so AI. Magnitude of margin = strength of signal.
  • Centroid distances are the easy sanity-check. to_ai ≪ to_human means the 7-number fingerprint is geometrically closer to AI-corpus centroid in standardized space. Here 0.022 vs 0.827 — a clean separation.
  • Basin entropy is the deepest signal. Human text trajectories spread across more attractor basins of the 26-mode eigenbasis. AI text collapses to fewer. Low entropy = AI-typical.
  • Surface features (formatting) were 0 here. That means the AI verdict comes entirely from structural dynamics, not from markdown/list/dash patterns. (This passage has none of those.)
  • The warning matters. 10 sentences is below the ~15-sentence reliability floor. Treat as suggestive, not definitive.

Method, in one paragraph

Waivelets computes a 7-number structural fingerprint from a text by (a) embedding each sentence with MiniLM-L6-v2 into ℝ³⁸⁴, (b) projecting through a 384×26 precomputed eigenbasis derived from wavelet analysis of a 100-text Gutenberg corpus, (c) measuring the trajectory's smoothness, oscillation, four domain activations (exposition/interiority/structure/narration), and basin entropy across the 26 attractors. The detector standardizes those 7 features against the corpus mean/std, applies a linear weight vector calibrated on 169 labeled samples (58 AI, 111 human), and outputs a composite score. Above the threshold = AI. The whole runtime is <1ms after embedding. Full whitepaper: /whitepaper.

Honest caveats. n=1. 10 sentences (below the 15-sentence reliability floor). Calibration set is small (169 samples). Waivelets and Pangram are reading different things — Pangram appears tuned to detect "AI-typical formality and hedge patterns," which informal lowercase prose evades. Waivelets reads embedding-space dynamics, which appears to catch this particular kind of AI voice that mimics human informality. The interesting result is the disagreement itself, not the verdict.

Why this might generalize

If waivelets really is reading structural dynamics that survive informal voice, it should also catch:

  • LLM-generated tweets and shitposts that mimic casual register
  • "Vibe-tuned" outputs from fine-tunes designed to sound human
  • RLHF artifacts in long-form prose — the basin-collapse signature

If it fails to catch those, the failure modes will tell us what dynamical signature is actually doing the work. Either way, more data is the only way to know. Send adversarial examples.
