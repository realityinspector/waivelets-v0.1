AI or human? And if AI — which model?

Paste any text below. The first stage (waivelets v0.1 AI detector, ~92.7% accuracy) decides AI vs human. If the verdict is AI, the second stage (v0.3 adversarial-basis classifier, ~88.5% leave-one-out 8-way accuracy) names the most likely model out of claude-sonnet-4 / claude-sonnet-4.5 / claude-haiku-4.5 / gpt-4-turbo / gpt-4o / gpt-5 / gemini-2.5-pro / llama-3.3-70b. Most reliable on ≥15 sentences.

Note on the probabilities below. The percentages express relative rank ordering, softened with a fixed temperature for legibility. They are NOT Platt-calibrated, so a "78% confident" call is not literally 78% likely to be right — it just means the top candidate is clearly preferred over the runner-up in the LDA's decision space. Treat the bars as a leaderboard, not a probability distribution.
